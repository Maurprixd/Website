PHASE 01

Ideation & Form

Mastay is a 2-in-1 kinetic piece — a compact storage tower that unfolds into a horizontal collaborative workspace. The trapezoid geometry of the Incan K'allku archway drove the form language: stable, structural, culturally grounded.

PHASE 02

Assembly Instructions

A full assembly brief was produced documenting the kinetic transformation sequence — from tower configuration to collaborative workspace. Industrial drawer sliders and high-strength hinges are the core mechanical components.
