PHASE 01

Ideation & Form

Meet the True Human — where anatomy meets innovation. Inspired by the structure of the human spine, this 3D-printed watch transforms organic movement into a sculptural, wearable form. Its interlocking segments and minimal face create a balance of futuristic design and human elegance.

Technical Specification

Detailed projections showing the top, front, and side profiles. This drawing ensures the interlocking segments match the anatomical curvature of the human wrist.
