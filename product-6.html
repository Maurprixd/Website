PHASE 01

Ideation & Form

Meet the True Human — where anatomy meets innovation. Inspired by the structure of the human spine, this 3D-printed watch transforms organic movement into a sculptural, wearable form. Its interlocking segments and minimal face create a balance of futuristic design and human elegance.

Concept

Spine-inspired interlocking geometry — each segment articulates at the wrist like a vertebra, derived from direct anatomical reference.

Material

FDM 3D-printed in multiple color iterations — physically worn and validated for flex and comfort at the wrist.

PHASE 02

Engineering & Scale

Technical orthographic views were generated in SolidWorks to establish precise dimensions for the 1:1 scale wearable prototype.

Technical Specification

Detailed projections showing the top, front, and side profiles. This drawing ensures the interlocking segments match the anatomical curvature of the human wrist.

  • Software SolidWorks
  • Units Millimeters (mm)
  • Prototype Scale 1:1 (Full Scale)
  • Material Metal (PLA Prototype)
