FLEED

PHASE 01

Ideation & Form

Fleed takes inspiration from the streamlined form of a bike helmet, combining aerodynamics with protection. Its smooth, compact body enhances efficiency, while the extended arms provide balance and stability blending movement with purpose in a farming context.

PHASE 02

Engineering

Technical orthographic and isometric projections generated in SolidWorks to establish precise manufacturing tolerances and define component assembly for the Fleed airframe.

Technical Specification

The assembly was engineered for high-stress agricultural environments, focusing on the integration of high-torque actuators and modular motor mounts.

  • Software SolidWorks 2024
  • Units mm (Millimeters)
  • Scale 1:1 (Full Scale)
  • Prototype Material 3D Printed
  • Final Materials Carbon Fiber & Aluminum
